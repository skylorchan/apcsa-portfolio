THE NATION OF SOLARIA

We, the founders of this country, have watched a nation where people work themselves sick and still cannot afford to see a doctor, where some live in mansions while others sleep on sidewalks, and where neighbors fear and shout at one another. We declare that no citizen of this country will ever be poor, ever be sick without care, ever go hungry, or ever feel afraid. Every person will be provided for, every person will be equal, and every person will be cared for, at every moment of every day. In our country, you will never have to worry again. We will do the worrying for you.
§1 · Zero SufferingA self-sustaining society engineered for 0% suffering. Work is optional and meant to be enjoyable.
§2 · Universal StipendEvery citizen receives 150 credits per day, up to a strict cap of 10,000. Nobody is poor. Nobody is rich.
§3 · AdmissionApplicants must be under 18 and pass the Citizenship Exam, plus genetic and drug screening. Launch population: 10,000.
§4 · Application EquityChildren born in Solaria are repatriated at birth so that every citizen earns their place the same way.
§5 · CompanionshipEvery citizen is assigned a cat or dog companion to hold baseline happiness at 100%.
§6 · HELIOSAn AGI oversees all cameras, all credits, and all Neuralinks. Infractions are fined. Major infractions are slimed.
